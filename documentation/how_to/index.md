# How-to guides

*Version 1.0 · 2026-10-15 · Status: current*

How-to guides are recipes for one task each. They assume you know what you want to do and list the steps to do it.

**Using the desktop app.**

- [Observatory control guide](../user_interface/user_guides/Observatory_Control_and_Automation_Guide.md): connect equipment and run the telescope from the app.

**Checking and tuning the processing.**

- [Tune the tool for your setup](tune_for_your_setup.md): an ordered checklist for checking the tuned limits on your own camera and telescope.
- [Validate a result on your data](validate_on_your_data.md): run a validation-register procedure on your own frames.

**Changing the code and its documentation.**

- [Change a tuned value](change_a_tuned_value.md): update the code, the register and the guides together.
- [Add a register entry](add_a_register_entry.md): record the evidence for a tuned value.
- [Add a quality gate](add_a_quality_gate.md): add a gate so the review guides and tests stay in step.
